Desi Merge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE OF FIRST RELEASE] · App: Desi Merge (com.desimerge.game) · Developer: [YOUR DEVELOPER NAME]

Summary

Desi Merge is an offline puzzle game. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not request the internet permission.

Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it does not use device identifiers or advertising IDs.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves game data only on your own device, inside the app's private storage: scores and best score, games played, items you have discovered, Diamonds, power-ups, skins, daily-reward and mission progress, a game in progress, and settings such as volume and language. This data never leaves your device through the game. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include the game's saved data in your personal device backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account settings, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. The game itself does not send this data anywhere.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. Google Play may collect information about app installs and crashes under Google's Privacy Policy; that is outside the game's control.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If the game ever adds a feature that uses personal data (for example online leaderboards or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
