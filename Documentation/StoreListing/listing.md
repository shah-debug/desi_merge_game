**Full description** (≤ 4000):

Milao • Barhao • Jeeto!

Desi Merge is a relaxing merge puzzle full of Pakistani flavour. Drop items into the jar, match two of the same, and
watch them merge into something bigger — from a tiny patti leaf to nimbu, tamatar, roti, aam, chai, mango shake,
rickshaw and the mighty bus.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag to aim, lift to drop.
• Two of the same item merge into the next one and score points.
• Keep the pile below the dashed line — if it stays above too long, the game ends.

FEATURES
• 50 hand-drawn desi items to collect across 5 skins: Desi Bazaar, Numbers, Mithai, Street Food and Truck Art.
• Hammer and Shuffle power-ups for tight spots.
• Daily rewards, daily and weekly missions, today's challenge and ranks to climb.
• Records, achievements and your personal score history.
• Play in English, Urdu (اردو), Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian or Malay.
• Fully offline — no account, no internet needed, no ads.
• Leave mid-game and continue right where you stopped.

Perfect for a quick chai break or a long relaxing session. Can you reach the bus?
